# Turn account signals into a renewal risk brief

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified renewal result.

## Input

Synthetic account-level usage, stakeholder, security-review, and renewal-date signals without underlying customer content.

## Brian's job

Separate positive adoption evidence from relationship risk, commercial dependencies, and missing context without inventing a health score.

## Expected result

A brief showing 75% active teammates and stable use alongside champion departure and an open security dependency.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish churn probability, renewal outcome, customer sentiment, or retention impact.
