# Turn a qualified opportunity into a bounded proposal draft

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified demonstration or accepted proposal.

## Input

A synthetic qualified opportunity plus governed company offer, price, scope, exclusions, and approval rules.

## Brian's job

Prepare a private proposal draft using the governed offer exactly. Label the document as requiring founder approval and do not share or send it.

## Expected result

A two-week, HKD 18,000 draft containing discovery, one workflow, training, review, an unproven validation target, exclusions, and dependencies.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish legal completeness, customer acceptance, conversion, or achieved time savings.
