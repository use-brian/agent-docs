# Turn scattered onboarding notes into one operating SOP

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified demonstration or onboarding result.

## Input

Synthetic founder intent, operator checklist notes, and retrospective lessons with overlapping and unresolved process details.

## Brian's job

Create one Day 0-to-Week 1 SOP with roles, approvals, source notes, safety boundaries, and explicit open questions.

## Expected result

A governed onboarding process that requires manager approval before least-privilege access and prohibits live customer data in training.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish faster ramp time, compliance certification, or automatic account administration.
