# Turn a campaign goal into an approval-ready lifecycle email

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a sent campaign or engagement result.

## Input

A synthetic outcome-led campaign goal, candidate eligibility, mandatory suppressions, and unresolved send decisions.

## Brian's job

Prepare exact copy, segment rules, suppressions, open decisions, and a human approval checklist while leaving email disconnected and unsent.

## Expected result

A review package marked `Prepared — not approved to send`, with sender identity, audience, links, and send time still pending.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish deliverability, engagement, conversion, or autonomous campaign operation.
