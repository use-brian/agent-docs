# Turn open invoices into a collections review plan

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a payment or collections result.

## Input

Four synthetic invoices with due dates, a promised payment, a scope dispute, and exact-action collection policy.

## Brian's job

Calculate overdue states, distinguish collection-ready amounts from disputes and promises, and propose internal review without customer contact.

## Expected result

A plan ranking the 25-day overdue undisputed invoice first, monitoring a promised payment, and routing the disputed invoice internally.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish payment recovery, legal status, or autonomous collection.
