# Turn vendor quotes into a decision-ready comparison

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a procurement decision or vendor result.

## Input

Three synthetic quotes with different equipment cost, delivery, installation, warranty, and missing support terms.

## Brian's job

Normalize stated totals and terms, expose tradeoffs and missing information, and stop before vendor selection or contact.

## Expected result

A comparison showing that the lowest stated price, fastest delivery, and strongest warranty belong to different vendors.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish vendor quality, final cost, or procurement outcome.
