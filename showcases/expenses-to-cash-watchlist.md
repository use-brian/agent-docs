# Turn expenses into a cash watchlist

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not financial, accounting, or tax advice.

## Input

A synthetic expense ledger and budget policy with paid, pending, uncertain, and missing-evidence items.

## Brian's job

Calculate budget exceptions, flag possible duplicates and missing receipts, and keep upcoming commitments separate from paid expenses.

## Expected result

A watchlist showing software HKD 3,200 over budget, a possible duplicate requiring verification, and no unsupported runway or tax claim.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish tax treatment, runway, accounting correctness, or savings.
