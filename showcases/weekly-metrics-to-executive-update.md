# Turn weekly metrics and notes into an executive update

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified demonstration or company-performance report.

## Input

A synthetic metrics table plus qualitative operating notes for one week.

## Brian's job

Calculate actual-versus-target gaps, separate facts from interpretations, identify decisions, and avoid inferring causality or a trend.

## Expected result

A private update showing MRR, activation, response-time, trial, and conversion gaps with source labels and two bounded decision requests.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish forecast accuracy, causal impact, business performance, or real company metrics.
