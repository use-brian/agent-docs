# Turn handover notes into a role continuity brief

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified handover outcome.

## Input

Synthetic notes containing recurring routines, dated commitments, governed decisions, active exceptions, and informal knowledge.

## Brian's job

Create a continuity brief that separates operating rhythm, next-seven-day commitments, approval boundaries, and missing ownership.

## Expected result

A reviewable role brief that flags the undocumented cold-room escalation owner and labels an informal vendor belief unverified.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish successful continuity or automatically reassign work.
