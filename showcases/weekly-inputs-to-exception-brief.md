# Turn three weekly updates into one exception brief

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified demonstration or customer result.

## Input

Three synthetic store updates containing revenue, stockout, staffing, and maintenance facts.

## Brian's job

Separate urgent exceptions from routine information, identify cross-store patterns, cite the source file beside every issue, and avoid inventing owners or deadlines.

## Expected result

A private brief prioritizing a cold-room risk, identifying an oat-milk stockout across exactly two stores, and keeping resolved or non-critical issues lower in the review order.

## Evidence boundary

This describes an intended multi-file synthesis. It does not establish automatic monitoring, incident resolution, time saved, or a real retail outcome.
