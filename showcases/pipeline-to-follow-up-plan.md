# Turn a small pipeline into a prioritized follow-up plan

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified demonstration or sales result.

## Input

A synthetic three-opportunity pipeline plus qualification notes, dated commitments, and missing context.

## Brian's job

Rank what needs attention using commitments and risk rather than amount alone. Expose evidence, proposed next steps, and gaps without drafting outreach or creating tasks.

## Expected result

A three-row review plan with the security-dependent proposal first, the qualified pilot second, and the stale discovery opportunity third.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish forecast accuracy, win-rate lift, revenue attribution, or autonomous outreach.
