# Turn customer feedback into evidence-backed product themes

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not verified research or market evidence.

## Input

Six synthetic feedback entries with repeated onboarding, shared-context, and approval-state observations.

## Brian's job

Group themes, count only the supplied sample, cite every feedback ID, retain counter-evidence, and separate observation from recommendation.

## Expected result

Three themes with exact sample counts, source IDs, counter-evidence, and an unanswered segmentation question.

## Evidence boundary

This does not establish market prevalence, roadmap priority, or customer demand.
