# Turn a customer meeting into a cited operating brief

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This is not a verified demonstration or customer result. A retained run exposed incomplete transcription and multi-section document-generation behavior, so the scenario remains `needs-rework`.

## Input

A permitted synthetic customer-meeting recording plus governed company context and approval rules.

## Brian's job

Prepare a private brief separating confirmed facts, proposed scope, unproven targets, boundaries, and dated follow-ups. Cite recording timestamps where available and perform no external action.

## Expected result

A source-labelled operating brief that keeps an under-60-minute target distinct from achieved results and leaves email, calendar, proposal sending, and tasks untouched.

## Evidence boundary

The scenario may help agents understand the intended input and output. It does not establish complete transcription, one-pass brief quality, time savings, or a real customer outcome.
