# Turn support tickets into a prioritized triage brief

> Approved for public scenario discovery. This prepared specification is not a verified support outcome.

## Input

A synthetic ticket pack mixing repeated access failures, a cosmetic issue, policy confusion, and expected exact-action approval behavior.

## Brian's job

Group related reports, rank urgency, distinguish likely defects from expected behavior, cite ticket IDs, and perform no support write.

## Expected result

The three-user calendar failure ranks first, while an approval-gated email is correctly labelled expected behavior rather than a defect.

## Evidence boundary

This does not confirm defects, establish response-time improvement, or resolve any customer issue.
